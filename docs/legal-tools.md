# RR-015E — Legal tools
The general interest calculator uses a fixed principal and user-entered fixed annual percentage rate. Dates are Gregorian calendar dates converted to UTC for elapsed-day arithmetic, excluding the start day; default dates use India local calendar date. Actual/365 convention is explicit. Simple interest P*r*d/365; compound P*expm1(n*d/365*log1p(r/n)), with annual, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly frequency and fractional periods. No legal determination, actual calendar posting, variable rates, part payments or inclusive start-day option. Amounts displayed to two decimals. Inputs are processed locally and not sent to a server by the calculator. Export is a plain-text arithmetic estimate, not an affidavit or certified claim schedule. Unit tests and browser tests run in CI. The site's global noindex launch gate remains active. Future MSMED and limitation tools must have independently verified statutory rules, authoritative rate data where relevant and explicit assumptions before release.

## Payment-aware MSME beta (supersedes unpaid-only limitation)
Record original invoice principal and dated payments assigned to that invoice. Each payment requires explicit principal-only, interest-only, principal-first or interest-first selection. These are user-selected arithmetic scenarios, not a legal rule of appropriation. Reject overpayments, dates outside acceptance through as-of, and missing allocation modes. Same-day entries processed in order; payment begins its date after any scheduled rest; interest within payments goes first to unposted accrual then capitalized interest. Capitalized interest remains interest (not invoice principal) and may continue to accrue in the model after principal is paid; verify applicability. Report separates original principal, principal paid, principal remaining, total interest earned, interest paid and outstanding interest. CSV includes payment allocations, rates and accrual intervals; numbers rounded for presentation, internal calculations unrounded. CSV opens in spreadsheet applications; native XLSX and dedicated PDF generation remain pending. Browser print is available. Beta/noindex remains. Automatic rates still require recorded coverage for the requested default window. No cross-invoice payment allocation is inferred.

## Date and duration calculator
General calendar arithmetic at /tools/date-calculator/. Supports dates from 1900 through 2200. UTC date-only arithmetic avoids timezone/DST effects; default dates are India-local today. Duration reports elapsed days, both-endpoints-inclusive days, weeks and days, and whole calendar months (grouped into years) plus residual days. Addition/subtraction combines years and months into one month shift clamped to the target month's last day, then applies weeks and days. Clamping is not reversible in all cases. Copy/text download, no network input transmission. No working-day count, holiday calendar, statutory exclusions, limitation or condonation determination. Reuse calendar engine for future legally verified calculators; statutory rules must be separate from arithmetic.
