# RR-015E — Legal tools
The general interest calculator uses a fixed principal and user-entered fixed annual percentage rate. Dates are Gregorian calendar dates converted to UTC for elapsed-day arithmetic, excluding the start day; default dates use India local calendar date. Actual/365 convention is explicit. Simple interest P*r*d/365; compound P*expm1(n*d/365*log1p(r/n)), with annual, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly frequency and fractional periods. No legal determination, actual calendar posting, variable rates, part payments or inclusive start-day option. Amounts displayed to two decimals. Inputs are processed locally and not sent to a server by the calculator. Export is a plain-text arithmetic estimate, not an affidavit or certified claim schedule. Unit tests and browser tests run in CI. The site's global noindex launch gate remains active. Future MSMED and limitation tools must have independently verified statutory rules, authoritative rate data where relevant and explicit assumptions before release.

## MSME estimator (beta)
The initial beta supports wholly unpaid invoices only, user-entered historical rate intervals with source references, acceptance-based due dates, and actual/365 daily accrual with monthly anniversary capitalization and an exclusive end date. These conventions are assumptions requiring review, not certified statutory rules. No partial payments, automatic bank-rate history or Excel export yet. Browser print-to-PDF is available. The page remains noindex even when site indexing is enabled. Tests use synthetic rates.

## RBI bank-rate history (source-transcribed beta)
Automatic mode uses src/data/rbi-bank-rates.json with 39 dated records, starting at MSMED commencement (2006-10-02), verified source coverage cutoff 2026-11-26, and explicit exclusive end 2026-11-27. The initial row is a coverage boundary, not a change date. Data was transcribed from the RBI 2025 and 2024 Handbook Table 43 Bank Rate column, earlier annual report and 2012 notification, and later 2025–26 MPC announcements. The RBI notification of 13 February 2012 specifies close of business; the model uses the handbook effective date at day granularity, not intraday accrual. Review this convention and dataset with a human reviewer before relying on results. Source links and periods are displayed at /tools/rbi-bank-rates/. No source extrapolation; manual mode remains available. Do not confuse Bank Rate with repo rate. Update via a reviewed commit after future policy changes; no automatic polling or human approval is claimed. Both pages remain beta and noindex.
