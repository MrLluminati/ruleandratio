# Design system

Approved direction: pure white background, deep navy typography, restrained gold accents. No courthouse, scales or gavels. The monogram and logo variants from the approved brand kit are the definitive artwork; RR-007 integrates simplified web vector outlines derived from the approved artwork, in public/brand/monogram.svg and wordmark.svg, with the tagline as accessible live text. The favicon uses the same monogram. Full-resolution print masters remain in the separate brand kit.

Tokens: navy #08223D; gold #C99B2B; white #FFFFFF; ink #202C38; muted #526172; border #DCE1E6; surface #F6F7F8; accessible dark gold #82600D. Gold is decorative, not small text on white. Georgia/Cambria serif for headlines and long reading; system sans for navigation and metadata. Maximum content width 1240px, article width 72ch, 4px spacing grid. Responsive single column for narrow screens, clear focus states, semantic heading hierarchy. No navy page backgrounds or glossy gradients.

## Editorial homepage
White masthead with horizontal logo lockup, compact responsive navigation, publication lead and editorial desk, knowledge library, discipline overview, journal feature, and editorial standards. No fake articles or misleading publication dates. Unpublished content is explicitly marked as pre-launch. The noindex gate remains in place. Search is deferred to RR-012.
