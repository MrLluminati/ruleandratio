# Criminal-law transition — editorial research guide

Draft only. The ordinary commencement of the three enactments was 1 July 2024. BNS Section 106(2) was excluded from original commencement; verify later notifications rather than assuming its present status. Official comparative resources: https://www.ncrb.mha.gov.in/uploads/SankalanPortal/Index.html .

For each matter record the date of alleged conduct, commencement of the relevant investigation, inquiry, trial, application or appeal, and the procedural order being challenged. Consider BNS Section 358 and Article 20(1) separately from BNSS Section 531 and BSA Section 170. Do not equate the date of FIR with the legal answer for all three statutes. Read the exact savings language and special legislation. Parvinder Singh v. Directorate of Enforcement, 2026 INSC 519, paras 26–29 and 36–39, addresses the distinction between administrative numbering and judicial inquiry in a PMLA complaint: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2025/44465/44465_2025_5_1501_71414_Judgement_19-May-2026.pdf .

Legacy section references in the draft collections are editorial search pointers, not a validated bidirectional conversion table. No claim of completeness, current-law certification, human review or publication is made.
