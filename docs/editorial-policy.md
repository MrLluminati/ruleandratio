# Editorial and professional safeguards

Working draft, pending final review. Primary-source accuracy is mandatory. Verify statutory text and commencement, judgment case name/citation/date/court, holding vs dicta, subsequent treatment, and applicable jurisdiction. Identify author, human reviewer, published date and last reviewed date. No fabricated citations, automated publication, unsupported legal claims or unmarked synthetic content.

Do not reproduce proprietary headnotes or protected editorial content. Observe reporting restrictions, privacy and confidentiality. Never commit client records, personal data or secrets to this public repository. Clearly distinguish information from advice. Maintain a corrections log, date corrections and periodically review time-sensitive material.

The publication is editorially distinct from Chambers of AK. No solicitation, misleading qualifications or paid placement disguised as editorial content. Obtain appropriate professional-conduct clarity before monetization. The future journal must have separate editorial governance, peer-review standards and transparent publication ethics before being described as peer reviewed or indexed.

## Discovery and attribution
Use the secondary-source policy in docs/content-acquisition.md. Produce independent legal analysis from authenticated documents; cite discovery sources when relied upon, respect publisher terms and copyright, and verify statutory reproduction rights (including Section 52(1)(q)(ii) of the Copyright Act). Do not publish confidential or restricted court material.
