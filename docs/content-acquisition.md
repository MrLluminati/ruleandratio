# RR-015A — Content acquisition

## Primary sources
Use the official India Code portal (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/), Legislative Department (https://legislative.gov.in/) and official Gazette portal (https://egazette.gov.in/). Use the actual document-specific URL in every record, not a homepage. For judgments use official court sources. Authenticate state laws through the relevant state gazette/department. Do not scrape competing publishers or their editorial headnotes. Check reuse rights, reporting restrictions and privacy.

## Draft import
1. Download an authenticated source document and retain it locally under .rr-sources/ (ignored by Git). Extract UTF-8 plain text using a suitable PDF/HTML extraction tool, and compare against the official document. Scanned sources require manual transcription/OCR checking. The importer does not fetch or OCR PDFs.
2. Copy docs/templates/import/manifest.json to the source directory. The sample is synthetic. Specify source document URLs, capture dates, source text filenames, legal subject IDs, chapter labels, provision numbers and headings. For every version specify source ID, one-based inclusive startLine/endLine, and original source pinpoint (page or provision). Inspect line boundaries before importing. Exclude running headers, page numbers, and unrelated material only with documented editorial review. Preserve provisos, explanations, illustrations, schedules and footnotes where legally relevant.
3. Run npm run import:statute -- .rr-sources/<act>/manifest.json . Output is .rr-import/<slug>/draft.json and review.json, never public content. Source text is copied without paraphrasing (line endings normalized to LF; UTF-8 decoding may remove BOM). The report records source-text SHA-256 and selected text SHA-256, capture dates and line mappings; these hashes prove local file identity, not official authenticity. Keep original PDFs and their checksums separately.
4. Review each provision against the original source and current legal position. Verify dates and amendments independently; missing dates are not inferred. Review report flags non-listed source hosts. It is not an authentication service. Do not include private material in the public repository.
5. After editorial approval, copy the reviewed JSON into src/content/statutes/<slug>.json, fill genuine reviewer, reviewedAt and status, retain selected-provisions coverage unless completeness is verified, and set draft:false only after approval. Archive provenance separately; the importer does not publish, overwrite or certify. Run npm run build, npm run test:import and npm run test:publishing. Commit only verified public material. Amendments are separately sourced, and the editor must validate every relationship.

## Secondary research and discovery sources
Bar & Bench (https://www.barandbench.com/), LiveLaw (https://www.livelaw.in/) and Indian Kanoon (https://indiankanoon.org/) are approved discovery sources for editorial ideas, trending legal issues, case identification and precedent research. PRS Legislative Research, PIB and Law Commission reports are additional research sources. A private publisher's report or headnote is secondary material; a hosted copy of a judgment may contain the primary legal text but must be authenticated against an official court copy where available. Record exact source URLs and cite secondary reporting relied upon. Do not treat a report as proof of a holding or rewrite its expression, headline, headnote or article structure as a substitute for original work. Distinguish oral observations, interim orders and final holdings. If original documents cannot be obtained, keep judgment analysis pending verification; attributed news reporting may be prepared as a draft pending appropriate evidence and editorial review.

Use manual browsing or explicitly permitted feeds/APIs in accordance with provider terms; do not bypass paywalls or access restrictions, bulk scrape or mirror proprietary databases. Indian Kanoon offers an API with separate terms and usage pricing; no paid integration is authorized by this policy. Review rights before republication. Section 52(1)(q)(ii) of the Copyright Act 1957 conditions the Act-reproduction exception on accompanying commentary or other original matter; design Bare Act pages with independently reviewed original explanatory material or establish another lawful basis. Section 52(1)(q)(iv) concerns judgments and orders unless publication is prohibited by the relevant judicial authority. Check reporting restrictions, privacy and third-party editorial copyright separately. Official reference: https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_xi.html . Provider terms: https://www.barandbench.com/terms-of-use ; https://www.livelaw.in/terms-and-conditions ; https://api.indiankanoon.org/terms/ .

## Licensed legal research databases
SCC OnLine (https://www.scconline.com/), Supreme Court Cases (SCC law reports), SCC OnLine Blog (https://www.scconline.com/blog/) and Manupatra (https://www.manupatra.com/) are additional approved research/discovery sources, subject to authorized access and applicable terms. Use them to identify authorities, verify reported and parallel citations, trace precedents and subsequent judicial treatment, and discover research topics. Verify holdings and paragraph references against authenticated judgments; confirm negative treatment against the later decisions themselves. Cite the relevant judgment and verified reporter citation, and attribute secondary commentary actually relied upon. Do not reproduce proprietary headnotes, digest notes, editorial enhancements, report facsimiles, database compilations or citator data in the public repository without an appropriate lawful basis and permission where required. No automated extraction, paywall bypass, account sharing or subscription purchase is authorized. Public official judgment texts and original Rule & Ratio analysis remain the preferred publication basis. Provider terms: https://www.scconline.com/TermsForGeneralUser.htm ; https://www.manupatra.com/asp/terms.aspx ; https://manupatra.com/Asp/copywrite.aspx .

## MSMED PDF extraction pilot
Run the independent GitHub workflow **MSMED source acquisition (draft only)** to attempt direct retrieval of the fixed official India Code URL. It generates a short-lived Actions review artifact, not a public website deployment or a repository content commit. The public repository and Actions artifacts must never be used for confidential material. If the official host denies CI access, manually obtain the original PDF and run `python3 scripts/extract_msmed.py <local-pdf>` with Python, Node and Poppler installed. The extraction is specific to the verified 13-page India Code layout, not a universal Act parser. It stops on unexpected headings or footers and preserves page-crossing Section 21. Original PDF hash, raw extracted pages, line ranges and draft JSON are recorded. Review every section against the official document and verify amendments, commencement and reproduction rights before adding any records to the published collection. No reviewer is assigned automatically. Source retrieval is a one-off action, not ongoing scraping.
