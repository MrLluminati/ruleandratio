## Legal framework

Identify the drawer, enforceable debt, instrument, presentment, reason for dishonour, demand and non-payment. Punishment can extend to two years of imprisonment, a fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both.

## Practical verification

Prepare a separate timeline for each cheque. Check current RBI validity directions rather than relying on the six-month statutory ceiling alone.

## Editorial checks

Verify current amendments, commencement, RBI directions where applicable, binding case law and the transition from the CrPC and Evidence Act to the BNSS and BSA. This is original explanatory commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a certified legal deadline. Human legal review remains pending.
