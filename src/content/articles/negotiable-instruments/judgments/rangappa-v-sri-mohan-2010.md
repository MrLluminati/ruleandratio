## Background
The High Court reversed an acquittal in a dispute concerning a dishonoured cheque and an alleged loan.

## Reasoning
The Supreme Court held that the Section 139 presumption encompasses the existence of an enforceable liability. The defence can rebut it by showing a probable alternative on the balance of probabilities. Existing prosecution materials may suffice; the accused need not invariably present independent evidence.

## Disposition and practical implications
The Court upheld the conviction on the facts. Distinguish the statutory evidential burden from the ultimate criminal standard. A bare denial does not establish a probable defence.
