## Question before the Court
The connected appeals concerned prosecution of persons associated with companies without joining the corporate principal as an accused.

## Reasoning and disposition
The three-judge Bench treated commission of the corporate offence as a prerequisite to derivative liability. It held that where the company can be prosecuted it must be arraigned; the responsibility of others depends on the statutory criteria. The cheque complaints in the relevant appeals were quashed. Paragraph 37 qualifies the earlier-authority discussion where legal impediments arise.

## Practical implications
Identify the drawer entity and the applicable basis of personal liability. Do not treat all directors as liable merely by designation. Check later authorities and any statutory impediment before invoking an exception.
