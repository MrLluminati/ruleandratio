## Background
The Sessions Court conditioned suspension of sentence on a deposit of 20% of the compensation. The High Court upheld the condition treating it as absolute.

## Reasoning and disposition
The Supreme Court recognised a normal rule of deposit, but allowed exceptional dispensation where the condition would be unjust or defeat the right of appeal. Specific reasons are required. The High Court orders were set aside and revisions restored for consideration, including whether payment had already been made.

## Practical implications
Document the facts supporting an exception and address the suspension application on its own terms. This does not abolish the deposit requirement or permit routine waiver. Check later judicial treatment, including the official judgment of 19 December 2024 listed in the research record.
