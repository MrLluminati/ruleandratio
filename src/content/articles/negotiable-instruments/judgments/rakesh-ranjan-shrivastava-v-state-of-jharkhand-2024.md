## Background
A Magistrate ordered interim compensation in a cheque prosecution arising from disputed business arrangements. The order survived challenges in the lower courts.

## Reasoning and disposition
The Supreme Court held the Section 143A power to be discretionary. The court must assess the apparent merits of the complaint and defence, relevant financial circumstances and the appropriate quantum, and provide brief reasons. The presumption alone does not justify the order. The application was remitted for fresh consideration.

## Practical implications
Address the merits and quantum independently. The appellate deposit regime under Section 148 concerns a convicted appellant and is not the test for pre-conviction compensation.
