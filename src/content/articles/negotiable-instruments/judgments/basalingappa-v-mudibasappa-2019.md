## Background
A complainant alleged a cash loan. The accused disputed the transaction and challenged the lender's ability to advance the money. The High Court reversed the trial court's acquittal.

## Reasoning
The Supreme Court explained the rebuttable nature of the presumption and the lower evidential standard applicable to the defence. A credible challenge arising from the complainant's own evidence may require the complainant to substantiate the alleged advance.

## Practical implications
The Court restored the acquittal. Financial capacity is a fact-sensitive issue, not an additional mandatory pleading in every complaint. Identify evidence supporting a genuine challenge rather than relying on a routine denial.
