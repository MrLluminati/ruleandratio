## Legal framework

The written demand must ordinarily be made within 30 days of receiving dishonour information from the bank. The drawer has 15 days after receipt to pay. Distinguish dispatch, actual service and legally deemed service.

## Practical verification

Keep the return memo, date of bank intimation, notice, postal receipts and tracking. Verify demand wording and compute the complaint deadline separately under Section 142.
