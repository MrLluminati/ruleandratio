## Legal framework

Identify whether the document records a promise or an order to pay, the parties and the conditions attached to payment. For electronic or truncated cheques, verify the statutory definition and banking process.

## Practical verification

Separate a mere receipt or acknowledgment of indebtedness from an instrument satisfying the required legal elements.
