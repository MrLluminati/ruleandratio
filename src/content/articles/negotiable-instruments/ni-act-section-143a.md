## Legal framework

The trial court has discretion to order compensation up to 20% of the cheque amount at the specified procedural stage. It must evaluate the prima facie case and relevant defence and give reasons.

## Practical verification

Check temporal applicability, stage, amount, financial circumstances and repayment consequences on acquittal. Do not confuse the ceiling with the Section 148 deposit floor. See Rakesh Ranjan Shrivastava.
