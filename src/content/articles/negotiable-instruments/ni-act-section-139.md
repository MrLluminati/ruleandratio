## Legal framework

Once the relevant foundational facts are established, the presumption extends to legally enforceable debt or liability, subject to rebuttal. The accused can raise a probable defence without necessarily giving personal testimony.

## Practical verification

Examine admissions, cross-examination, documentary contradictions and the complainant's financial capacity where genuinely disputed. See Rangappa and Basalingappa.
