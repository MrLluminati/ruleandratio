## Legal framework

Identify the corporate drawer and each individual's statutory basis of responsibility at the relevant time. Corporate designation alone is not sufficient for every category of accused.

## Practical verification

Verify company identity, signatory authority, relevant role and complaint averments; consider the requirement to arraign the company and legally recognised exceptions. See Aneeta Hada.

## Editorial checks

Verify current amendments, commencement, RBI directions where applicable, binding case law and the transition from the CrPC and Evidence Act to the BNSS and BSA. This is original explanatory commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a certified legal deadline. Human legal review remains pending.
