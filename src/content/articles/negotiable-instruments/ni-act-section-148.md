## Legal framework

Where ordered, the deposit is at least 20% of the fine or compensation awarded, additional to Section 143A payments. Courts generally impose the statutory deposit but may recognise exceptional cases with recorded reasons.

## Practical verification

Identify the actual award, deposit order, payment deadline, any release and exceptional grounds for dispensation. See Jamboo Bhandari; verify later treatment.
