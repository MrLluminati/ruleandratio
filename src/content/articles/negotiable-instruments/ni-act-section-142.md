## Legal framework

The payee or holder in due course ordinarily brings the written complaint within one month after the cause of action arises under Section 138(c). The court can consider sufficient cause for delayed filing under the statutory proviso.

## Practical verification

Distinguish the 15-day payment opportunity from the one-month complaint period; a month is not interchangeable with 30 days. Verify competent Magistrate and authorisation.
