## Legal framework

Examine the circumstances in which a signed incomplete stamped instrument was delivered and the scope of authority to complete it.

## Practical verification

Do not automatically apply the stamped-instrument rule to every blank cheque. For cheque disputes separately examine execution, voluntary delivery and the presumptions under Sections 118 and 139.
