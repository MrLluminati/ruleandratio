## Legal framework

Examine cancellation, release, payment and alteration of the instrument. Check consent and whether the alteration implements the parties' original intention.

## Practical verification

Compare the original document with copies and preserve bank records. Distinguish authorised completion of a blank from an unauthorised material alteration.
