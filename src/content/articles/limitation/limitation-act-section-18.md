## Legal framework

Identify an attributable signed acknowledgment made while the earlier period remained alive. Examine the admission in its full context.

## Practical verification

Record signing dates and qualifications in accounts or correspondence; do not assume a later demand letter or a post-expiry acknowledgment restarts limitation. See the Bishal Jaiswal draft.

Check current central and local amendments, special-law exclusions, binding precedent and applicable court rules, including any exceptional judicial exclusion orders. This is explanatory commentary rather than statutory text or a certified deadline calculation. Human legal review pending.
