## Legal framework

The ordinary period is three years from accrual of the right to apply, when no specific applicable period governs.

## Practical verification

Determine the forum, enabling statute and nature of the application before using the residuary article. A special statutory deadline may displace it.

## Editorial caution

Check current central and local amendments, special-law exclusions, binding precedent and applicable court rules, including any exceptional judicial exclusion orders. This is explanatory commentary rather than statutory text or a certified deadline calculation. Human legal review pending.
