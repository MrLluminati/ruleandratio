## Legal framework

Identify whether a qualifying disability existed when the entitlement arose, the nature of the proceeding, and any statutory cap. A later disability does not ordinarily interrupt a period already running.

## Practical verification

Check joint entitlements, successive disabilities and the date the disability ceased; avoid treating every personal hardship as statutory disability.

## Editorial caution

Check current central and local amendments, special-law exclusions, binding precedent and applicable court rules, including any exceptional judicial exclusion orders. This is explanatory commentary rather than statutory text or a certified deadline calculation. Human legal review pending.
