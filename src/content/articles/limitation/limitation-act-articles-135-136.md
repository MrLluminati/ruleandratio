## Legal framework

Mandatory injunctions generally carry a three-year period; other covered civil decrees generally carry twelve years. Perpetual injunctions have a specific exception.

## Practical verification

Check enforceability, fixed or recurring performance dates and default, any stay and whether the relief is a mandatory or perpetual injunction.

## Editorial caution

Check current central and local amendments, special-law exclusions, binding precedent and applicable court rules, including any exceptional judicial exclusion orders. This is explanatory commentary rather than statutory text or a certified deadline calculation. Human legal review pending.
