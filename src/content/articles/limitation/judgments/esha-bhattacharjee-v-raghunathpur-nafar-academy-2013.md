## Background
A school management sought to appeal an order protecting a teacher's employment after prolonged non-compliance and substantial delay.

## Decision
The Supreme Court reversed the condonation order. Its analysis calls for a practical approach attentive to diligence, credibility, prejudice and the circumstances of the delay. Substantial justice does not require courts to accept an implausible explanation.

## Practical implications
A condonation application should explain what happened and why the litigant could not act sooner. The length of delay is relevant but not the sole test. Distinguish genuine inability to act from deliberate inaction.
