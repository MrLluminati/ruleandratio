## Background
The appellants challenged dismissal of delayed land-acquisition appeals. Illness was among the explanations advanced.

## Reasoning and disposition
The Supreme Court held that equitable considerations cannot substitute for an adequate explanation under the applicable statute. It upheld refusal to condone the delay. The case recognises a liberal but principled approach rather than an unrestricted discretion.

## Practical implications
Document the causal relationship between the impediment and the missed filing date. A waiver of interest or an offer of costs cannot by itself cure the absence of sufficient cause.
