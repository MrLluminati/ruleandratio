## Background
An insolvency application raised a limitation dispute about whether financial statements could acknowledge an existing debt.

## Reasoning
The Supreme Court rejected a categorical rule that mandatory preparation of financial statements prevents a balance sheet from acknowledging liability. The treatment of a particular entry depends on its language and accompanying qualifications. The connected matters required adjudication applying that principle.

## Practical implications
Review the entire signed financial statement, notes and audit qualifications together with dates and the original limitation chronology. The decision does not make every accounting entry an acknowledgment, nor remove the requirement that it be made before the relevant period expires.
