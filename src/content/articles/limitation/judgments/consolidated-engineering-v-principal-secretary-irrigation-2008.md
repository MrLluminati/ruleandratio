## Background
Two arbitration challenges had been pursued in courts without jurisdiction before reaching the proper forum.

## Reasoning
The Court held that Section 14 of the Limitation Act is not excluded by the strict filing regime under Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act. The statutory exclusion operates in computing the period, rather than expanding the discretionary power to excuse late filing. The Court found the prerequisites satisfied on the facts and directed further proceedings.

## Practical implications
Collect proof of the earlier filing, diligent pursuit, jurisdictional defect and subsequent presentation. The judgment does not permit a court to condone delay without limit; exclusion depends on demonstrating the statutory conditions.
