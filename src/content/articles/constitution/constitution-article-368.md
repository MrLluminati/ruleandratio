## Legal framework

Distinguish ordinary lawmaking, constituent amendment and the cases requiring State ratification. Examine substantive constitutional limits independently of procedural compliance.

## Practical verification

Identify the exact amendment and its effect on the constitutional feature asserted. Read Kesavananda and subsequent authorities rather than using a fixed checklist of basic features.

## Editorial checks

Check the official text, amendment and commencement history, applicable orders, current binding interpretations and subsequent judicial treatment. Printed provisions may be affected by judicial invalidation or reading down. This is original explanatory commentary, not verbatim constitutional text. Human legal review is pending.
