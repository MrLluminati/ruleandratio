## Legal framework

Identify the enabling legislation and tribunal jurisdiction. Distinguish first-instance adjudication from High Court constitutional review.

## Practical verification

Check the tribunal's power to examine the issue and the applicable judicial route. Read L. Chandra Kumar alongside the actual tribunal statute.

## Editorial checks

Check the official text, amendment and commencement history, applicable orders, current binding interpretations and subsequent judicial treatment.
