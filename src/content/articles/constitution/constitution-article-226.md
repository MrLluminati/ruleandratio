## Legal framework

Identify the legal or fundamental right, respondent's public duty where relevant, territorial nexus and appropriate relief. The existence of a statutory remedy may affect discretionary entertainment.

## Practical verification

Address alternative remedies and any recognised exception with concrete facts. Distinguish mandamus, certiorari, prohibition, habeas corpus and quo warranto. See Whirlpool.
