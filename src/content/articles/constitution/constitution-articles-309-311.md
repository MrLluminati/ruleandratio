## Legal framework

Identify the service rules, status of the employee, competent appointing authority and nature of the disciplinary action. Distinguish constitutional protection from additional statutory rights.

## Practical verification

Check charge documents, inquiry record, opportunity of hearing and any exception claimed. For legislative secretariat staff also examine the special constitutional framework.

## Editorial checks

Check the official text, amendment and commencement history, applicable orders, current binding interpretations and subsequent judicial treatment. Printed provisions may be affected by judicial invalidation or reading down. This is original explanatory commentary, not verbatim constitutional text. Human legal review is pending.
