## Legal framework

Separate admission or establishment of States from changes to existing State boundaries. Examine the applicable parliamentary procedure and presidential reference requirements.

## Practical verification

Check whether the measure concerns domestic reorganisation or transfer of territory to another country, which raises distinct constitutional questions.
