## Legal framework

Identify the interference with liberty or dignity and its legal basis. Examine fairness of procedure and the applicable constitutional standard, including proportionality where relevant.

## Practical verification

For a privacy claim, identify what information or autonomy is affected and the safeguards governing the interference. See Puttaswamy.
