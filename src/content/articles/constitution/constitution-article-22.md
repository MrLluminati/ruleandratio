## Legal framework

Distinguish ordinary arrest from preventive detention and assess the applicable safeguards and exceptions. Identify the legal authority for continued custody.

## Practical verification

Obtain arrest and detention records, grounds communicated, representation documents and relevant dates. Verify the specific detention statute and current criminal procedure.

## Editorial checks

Check the official text, amendment and commencement history, applicable orders, current binding interpretations and subsequent judicial treatment.
