## Legal framework

Identify the entity responsible for the challenged action and whether it falls within the relevant constitutional test. Distinguish invalidity of ordinary law from review of constitutional amendments.

## Practical verification

For a statutory body or government-controlled entity, gather its constituting instrument, functions and evidence of control. A public-duty writ inquiry may differ from Article 12 status.

## Editorial checks

Check the official text, amendment and commencement history, applicable orders, current binding interpretations and subsequent judicial treatment. Printed provisions may be affected by judicial invalidation or reading down. This is original explanatory commentary, not verbatim constitutional text. Human legal review is pending.
