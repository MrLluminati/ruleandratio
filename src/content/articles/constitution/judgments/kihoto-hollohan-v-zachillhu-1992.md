## Context
Petitions challenged the constitutional validity of the anti-defection amendment and exclusion of court jurisdiction.

## Majority reasoning
The majority preserved the disqualification scheme but severed the jurisdiction-excluding paragraph 7 because the required State ratification had not occurred. The presiding officer's adjudicatory determination remains subject to judicial review on limited grounds. The minority would have invalidated the entire amendment.

## Scope and review
Distinguish final disqualification decisions from exceptional intervention during pending proceedings. Do not infer that the case settles every issue of standing or delay. Check later amendments, House rules and subsequent case law.
