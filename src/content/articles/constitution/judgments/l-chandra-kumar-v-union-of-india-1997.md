## Context
The Court considered provisions transferring adjudication to statutory tribunals and excluding the constitutional courts' jurisdiction.

## Decision
The seven-judge Bench preserved constitutional judicial review as a structural safeguard and rejected the relevant exclusion clauses to that extent. Tribunals may supplement the superior courts rather than replace them. Their decisions are ordinarily reviewable before a Division Bench of the relevant High Court.

## Scope
Tribunals generally remain the first forum for matters within their assigned domain, subject to the qualification concerning challenges to their parent enactments. Check the enabling statute, subsequent authorities and proper procedural route.
