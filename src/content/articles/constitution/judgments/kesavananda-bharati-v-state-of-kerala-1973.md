## Context
Challenges to land reform measures developed into a dispute about the extent of Parliament's constituent power.

## Controlling principle
The thirteen-judge Bench produced separate opinions. The decisive majority accepted a broad power to amend but not to destroy the Constitution's basic structure. The individual opinions differ on the treatment of particular amendments and on the formulation of protected features.

## Scope and review
For a later challenge, distinguish procedural defects in an amendment from its substantive effect on constitutional identity. Do not present a single judge's list as an exhaustive majority holding. Verify the signed opinions and later decisions on the specific feature asserted.
