## Context
The Aadhaar litigation prompted a reference on whether the Constitution protects individual privacy.

## Decision
All nine judges recognised constitutional privacy protection. The opinions connect privacy to personal liberty, dignity and other rights in Part III. Interference requires justification; the opinions examine legality, legitimate objectives and proportionality, with differences in formulation.

## Scope
The reference did not decide the overall validity of the Aadhaar programme. Assess the kind of intrusion and applicable legal safeguards, and read later decisions on the particular right or scheme. Distinguish this 2017 ruling from the 2018 Aadhaar merits judgment.
