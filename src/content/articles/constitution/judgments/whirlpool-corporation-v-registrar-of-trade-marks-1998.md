## Context
A trade mark dispute involved a challenge to administrative action despite a statutory remedial mechanism.

## Reasoning
The Court distinguished the High Court's constitutional power from its usual restraint when an effective alternative remedy exists. A serious jurisdictional defect, breach of natural justice or a Fundamental Rights claim may justify entertainment despite another remedy.

## Practical relevance
Identify the precise statutory appeal or review and the factual basis of the exception claimed. A generic assertion of unfairness does not establish the exception; the constitutional remedy remains discretionary. Check later decisions in the relevant statutory context.
