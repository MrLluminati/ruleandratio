## Context
The petitions concerned a change of government in Maharashtra, competing party factions and the exercise of constitutional powers.

## Reasoning and relief
The Court distinguished the political party from its legislative wing for defection purposes. It found the Governor lacked adequate objective grounds for the particular confidence vote, but declined restoration of the former ministry after the Chief Minister's voluntary resignation. The Speaker was to determine pending disqualifications.

## Open question
The Court referred the Nabam Rebia issue concerning a notice to remove the Speaker to a larger Bench. Verify subsequent disposition before stating the current law. This judgment is not a general ruling about pre-oath membership or the standing of non-members under particular House rules.
