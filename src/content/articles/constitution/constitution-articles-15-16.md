## Legal framework

Separate the grounds of prohibited discrimination from enabling provisions for affirmative action. Public employment raises distinct requirements under Article 16.

## Practical verification

Check the applicable reservation category, recruitment rules, roster, eligibility date and current case law; avoid transferring rules for one category to another.
