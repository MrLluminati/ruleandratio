## Background
A three-judge reference addressed proof of electronic records under the Indian Evidence Act in connected appeals.

## Decision
The Court affirmed the mandatory certification rule for electronic records tendered under the former Section 65B, distinguishing the production and proof of original electronic records. It explained the possibility of obtaining the required certificate during proceedings, subject to fairness and procedural constraints.

## Scope
This is an Evidence Act decision. BSA Sections 57 and 61–63 and the statutory certificate Schedule must be considered independently where the new law applies. Do not copy the old certificate format without checking the new requirements.
