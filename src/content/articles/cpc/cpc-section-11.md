## Procedural overview

Compare pleadings, issues and findings in the earlier case with the later dispute. Record finality, competence and parties or privies. Constructive res judicata requires examination of matters that might and ought to have been raised.

## Practical application

A prior decree is not by itself proof of every element of the bar; examine the underlying record. See the draft case note on Pandurangan.
