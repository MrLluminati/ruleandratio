## Background
In specific-performance suits over proposed land sales, the courts below had restrained further dealings with the properties. Whether negotiations had produced a concluded contract was disputed.

## Reasoning
The Supreme Court considered the strength of the preliminary case alongside the comparative harm, adequacy of damages and the applicant's conduct. Negotiation correspondence had to be considered in context; its ultimate evidentiary meaning remained for trial.

## Scope
An interim hearing is not the occasion for definitive findings on disputed contractual formation. A request to preserve property should address the substantive claim and equitable considerations, not only the possibility of a transfer. Check the Specific Relief Act amendments applicable to the particular transaction before generalising observations about specific performance.
