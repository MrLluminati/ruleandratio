## Background
The plaintiffs challenged registered conveyances, alleging unpaid sale consideration. The trial court rejected the plaint; the High Court affirmed.

## Decision and reasoning
The Supreme Court examined the threshold grounds for rejection under Rule 11(a) and (d). It assessed the pleading and its supporting documents, rather than conducting a full trial. On the pleaded chronology the challenge was time-barred, and the Court found no sustainable basis for the claimed cancellation. The appeal was dismissed with costs.

## Practical scope
Distinguish a bar apparent on the plaintiff's own case from limitation dependent on contested facts. Identify the necessary material facts and avoid relying on disputed defence evidence in a Rule 11 application.
