## Background
Execution of property decrees had been delayed by repeated objections and related proceedings. The Supreme Court addressed both the particular appeals and systemic procedural delays.

## Directions
The Court emphasised clear executable decrees, early identification of property disputes and scrutiny of repetitive or frivolous execution objections. It directed disposal of execution proceedings within six months of filing, subject to extension supported by recorded reasons.

## Scope
The directions do not extinguish legitimate third-party claims or impose an automatic expiry of execution after six months. Check later decisions and the applicable High Court execution rules. The appeals were dismissed.
