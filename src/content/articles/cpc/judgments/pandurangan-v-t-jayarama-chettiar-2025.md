## Background
A purchaser sought declaration of title and injunction, alleging that an earlier ex parte partition decree was collusive. The trial court rejected the plaint on res judicata grounds and the High Court declined interference.

## Reasoning
The Supreme Court relied on Srihari Hanumandas Totala and other authorities distinguishing the threshold examination of a plaint from an inquiry into the earlier litigation. The alleged collusion and effect of the prior decree required further examination.

## Disposition and limits
The appeal succeeded and the suit was restored. The Court deliberately left the merits of the res judicata defence open. The decision concerns the proper procedural stage for examining that defence, rather than declaring the earlier decree invalid.
