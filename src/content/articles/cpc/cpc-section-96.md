## Procedural overview

Identify the decree and appeal forum, any statutory exclusion and applicable limitation. An original decree passed ex parte may be appealable; a consent decree is subject to the statutory bar.

## Practical application

Contrast an appeal on facts and law with a second appeal confined to substantial questions of law under Section 100.
