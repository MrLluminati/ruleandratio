## Procedural overview

Check identity and capacity of the defendant, the challenged official act and whether statutory notice is required. For urgent relief examine the leave mechanism under subsection (2) and the opportunity for the government or officer to respond.

## Practical application

Do not treat urgency as a blanket exemption; document notice service, contents and the relevant court order.
