## Procedural overview

Start by identifying the civil right and remedy sought. Check whether the governing special statute excludes ordinary civil jurisdiction expressly or by necessary implication. A tribunal's existence alone does not establish exclusion.

## Practical application

A suit challenging a statutory authority's decision requires examination of the statutory remedial scheme, not simply the label attached to the relief.
