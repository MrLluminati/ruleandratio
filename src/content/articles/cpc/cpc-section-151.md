## Procedural overview

Identify why an order is necessary for justice or to prevent abuse of court process. Inherent jurisdiction cannot be used to bypass an express statutory restriction.

## Practical application

Distinguish a procedural gap from a request to circumvent a specific appeal, review or limitation provision.
