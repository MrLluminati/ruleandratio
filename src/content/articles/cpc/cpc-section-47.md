## Procedural overview

Examine whether the question concerns execution, discharge or satisfaction of the decree and falls between parties or their representatives. Such issues are decided in execution rather than through a fresh suit.

## Practical application

Separate disputes about implementing the decree from attempts to reopen its merits; identify the procedure for genuine third-party claims.
