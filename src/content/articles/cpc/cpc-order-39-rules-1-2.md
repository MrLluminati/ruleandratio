## Procedural overview

Identify the threatened injury and requested restraint. Address prima facie case, comparative hardship and injury not adequately compensable in damages. A temporary order should not decide disputed final rights unnecessarily.

## Practical application

For an ex parte request, separately check Rule 3 notice requirements and reasons for dispensing with notice.
