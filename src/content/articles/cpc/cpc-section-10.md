## Procedural overview

Compare the pending earlier suit, parties, title under which they litigate, issues and competence of the first court. The provision concerns stay of trial, not automatic rejection of the plaint.

## Practical application

Distinguish an existing pending proceeding from a finally decided proceeding, which raises a different question under Section 11.
