## Procedural overview

Explain the proposed amendment, its relevance to resolving the dispute, prejudice to the other side and the procedural stage. After commencement of trial the due-diligence proviso requires separate consideration.

## Practical application

Check whether the amendment introduces a new claim, changes the case or raises a limitation issue.
