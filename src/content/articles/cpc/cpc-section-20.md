## Procedural overview

Identify the defendant's relevant residence or business and the location of material events constituting the cause of action. Consider Sections 15–19 first and any applicable special jurisdiction statute.

## Practical application

A contractual forum clause cannot create jurisdiction in a court otherwise lacking it. Verify the clause and actual connecting facts.
