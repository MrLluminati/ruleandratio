## Procedural overview

Record the date of service of summons and applicable procedural regime. Ordinary civil litigation and commercial disputes of specified value have different treatment of filing deadlines.

## Practical application

Do not transfer the strict commercial-dispute forfeiture rule to every civil suit. Check the Commercial Courts Act amendments and governing precedent.
