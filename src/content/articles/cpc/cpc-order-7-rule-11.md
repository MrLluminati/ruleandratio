## Procedural overview

Identify the precise enumerated ground. Read the plaint as a whole and relevant documents relied upon in it; do not try disputed defences at this stage.

## Practical application

Distinguish rejection of a plaint from return under Rule 10 and dismissal after trial. A rejection is treated as a decree under Section 2(2).
