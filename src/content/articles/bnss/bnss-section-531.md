## Legal framework and practical verification

Identify proceedings actually pending immediately before commencement, their stage and the nature of the right asserted. Parvinder Singh distinguishes administrative registration from judicial inquiry; do not choose procedural law solely from the FIR date.

## Legacy-law research reference

Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973: CrPC repeal; no direct equivalence. This is a research pointer, not a declaration of identical ingredients, procedure or legal effect. Compare the full texts, amendments, commencement and savings before relying on a legacy precedent.
