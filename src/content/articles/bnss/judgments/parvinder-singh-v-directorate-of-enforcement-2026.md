## Background
A PMLA complaint was filed before the BNSS commenced, but cognizance was taken after commencement without hearing the accused.

## Decision
The Supreme Court held that merely numbering the complaint and listing it for cognizance did not constitute a pending judicial inquiry. In this setting the first proviso to Section 223(1) applied; failure to hear the accused invalidated the cognizance order. The Court explained that Section 531 avoids splitting saved proceedings between old and new procedural codes.

## Scope
Examine actual judicial steps before commencement and the special statute involved. Do not treat the decision as resolving every transition question or every form of prosecution.
