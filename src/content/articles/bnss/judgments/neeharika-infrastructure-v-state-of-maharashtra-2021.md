## Background
The appeal concerned interim protection against coercive measures during a pending FIR-quashing petition.

## Decision
The Supreme Court set aside the interim direction without deciding the allegations on merits. It emphasised restraint during investigation, exceptional and reasoned interim interference, and clarity about the scope of any protective order.

## Scope
The judgment interpreted CrPC Section 482 and Article 226. BNSS Sections 528 and 482 are relevant research counterparts for quashing and anticipatory bail respectively, but verify applicable savings and later judicial treatment.
