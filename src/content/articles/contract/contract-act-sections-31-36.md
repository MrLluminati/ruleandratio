## Legal framework

Identify the uncertain collateral event and the agreed consequences of its occurrence or non-occurrence.

## Practical verification

For force majeure, analyse the clause, notice, exclusions and contractual remedy before invoking frustration outside the contract. See Energy Watchdog.
