## Legal framework

Identify the fact or legal rule allegedly misunderstood and whether the misunderstanding was shared. A mistake about an essential fact by both parties differs from unilateral error.

## Practical verification

A poor estimate of commercial value is not automatically a qualifying mistake. Examine the allocation of risk and independent grounds for relief.

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
