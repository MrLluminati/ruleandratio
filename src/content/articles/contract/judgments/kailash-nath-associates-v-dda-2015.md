## Background
The dispute arose from forfeiture of earnest money following a development authority's land auction and subsequent resale.

## Reasoning and outcome
The Court restored the refund decision. It found no contractual breach by the bidder or compensable loss on the facts. Section 74 limits recovery to reasonable compensation within the stipulated ceiling; ascertainable losses require supporting proof, while a genuine estimate can be relevant when quantification is difficult.

## Practical limits
Identify whether a contract had already arisen at forfeiture and distinguish the decision's treatment of pre-contract auction conditions. A penalty clause is not an automatic debt.
