## Background
A bank obtained a decree against a debtor and a guarantor, but the lower court restricted enforcement against the guarantor until other recovery avenues were exhausted.

## Reasoning and outcome
The Supreme Court removed that restriction. Subject to agreed limitations and any applicable special equity, the guarantor's obligation is not postponed merely because the debtor can pay. Payment allows the surety to exercise subrogation rights against the debtor.

## Practical limits
Examine the guarantee's invocation conditions and any statutory moratorium or discharge defence independently. The general rule does not override express terms or special legislation.
