## Legal framework

Examine clauses limiting access to courts, shortening enforcement windows or extinguishing rights, and check the relevant statutory exceptions.

## Practical verification

Distinguish a valid selection among competent forums and an arbitration agreement from an impermissible restriction. Review the version applicable on the contract date.

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
