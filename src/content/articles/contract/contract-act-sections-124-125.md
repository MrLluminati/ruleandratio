## Legal framework

Identify the indemnified risk, trigger and scope of recoverable amounts. Consider the terms and applicable judicial interpretation.

## Practical verification

Check notice, defence control, settlement consent and liability already incurred; do not assume actual payment is invariably required before relief.

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
