## Legal framework

Identify the breach and contractual ceiling; assess reasonable compensation rather than assuming automatic entitlement to the stipulated amount.

## Practical verification

Distinguish loss capable of proof from loss difficult to quantify. Examine whether the sum genuinely estimates loss and whether the forfeiture follows a concluded agreement. See Kailash Nath.
