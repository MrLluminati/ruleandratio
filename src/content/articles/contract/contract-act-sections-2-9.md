## Legal framework

Trace the exchange of proposals and responses. Distinguish an unconditional acceptance from a counteroffer, and identify when communication and revocation became effective.

## Practical verification

Preserve original correspondence and attachments; determine whether execution of a later formal agreement was a condition of binding assent. See Trimex.

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
