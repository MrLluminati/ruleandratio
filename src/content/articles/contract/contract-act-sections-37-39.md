## Legal framework

Identify the obligation due, any valid tender and the nature of the other party's refusal. Consider whether conduct amounts to acquiescence in continued performance.

## Practical verification

Keep evidence of readiness, notices and elections to terminate or continue. Separate repudiation from a remediable delay.
