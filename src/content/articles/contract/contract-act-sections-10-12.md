## Legal framework

Examine consent, consideration, object, capacity and any independent statutory formality. Capacity must be assessed at the time of contracting.

## Practical verification

For a company or partnership, separately verify authority of the person signing; for an individual check age and applicable capacity rules.

## Editorial checks

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
