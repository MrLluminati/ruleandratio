## Legal framework

Identify creditor, principal debtor and surety; distinguish a single guarantee from one covering a series of transactions. Examine any contractual limit on liability.

## Practical verification

Check invocation requirements and caps. Ordinarily the creditor need not first exhaust remedies against the debtor. See Bank of Bihar.
