## Legal framework

Identify whether material obligations are ascertainable and whether a transaction is a wager rather than a genuine commercial risk allocation.

## Practical verification

Check agreed pricing mechanisms and applicable special or state legislation; distinguish uncertainty from a dispute over construction.

## Editorial checks

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
