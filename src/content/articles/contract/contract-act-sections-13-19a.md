## Legal framework

Distinguish absence of consensus from consent affected by an alleged vitiating factor. Identify the specific inducement and its causal effect on agreement.

## Practical verification

Collect contemporaneous evidence of representations, relationship, pressure and bargaining circumstances. Check available rescission and restitution remedies.

## Editorial checks

Confirm applicable statutory text, amendments, special legislation, binding precedent and genuine human legal review. This is original commentary, not verbatim statutory text or a determination of any particular dispute.
