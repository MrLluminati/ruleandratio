## Legal framework

Build a claim around breach, causation, recoverable loss and mitigation. Distinguish losses arising ordinarily or contemplated at formation from remote losses.

## Practical verification

Prepare separate evidence for each damages head and explain avoided costs; a damages formula is not necessarily proof of actual loss.
