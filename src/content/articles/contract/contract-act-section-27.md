## Legal framework

Identify the scope, duration and purpose of a restraint. Consider the goodwill-sale exception and the distinction between restraints during contractual performance and after termination.

## Practical verification

Separate a non-compete from confidentiality, intellectual-property and non-solicitation obligations; verify applicable authorities rather than assuming every restriction is enforceable.
