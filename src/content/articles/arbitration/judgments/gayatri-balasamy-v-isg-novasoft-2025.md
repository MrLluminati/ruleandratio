## Reference
The Constitution Bench considered whether judicial power over an arbitral award extends beyond annulment to modification. The majority opinion was signed by four judges; a separate dissent accompanies it.

## Majority conclusion
Paragraph 85 recognises narrowly defined intervention: removal of an independently separable invalid part, correction of obvious clerical or numerical mistakes, and adjustment of interest accruing after the award in specified circumstances. It separately recognises the Supreme Court's restrained constitutional power under Article 142.

## Limits of the ruling
A challenge is not a fresh assessment of entitlement or quantum. An ordinary court cannot invoke Article 142, and a request to enhance damages based on contested evidence must not be presented as a mere correction.
