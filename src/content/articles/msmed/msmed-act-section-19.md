## Challenge to an award

The statutory deposit requirement concerns a challenger other than the supplier. The court has a role in directing the deposit and determining interim payment to the supplier.

## Review points

Identify the challenged decision and applicable procedural route. Check limitation, the amount payable, the court's directions and binding authorities before filing. Do not confuse deposit compliance with automatic grant of a stay.
