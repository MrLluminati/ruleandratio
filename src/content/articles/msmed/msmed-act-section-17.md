## What Section 17 covers

Section 17 connects the amount due for goods or services supplied by an eligible supplier with the interest payable under Section 16. It is a substantive recovery provision, not an independent guarantee that every invoice or damages claim is payable.

## Prepare a reconciliation

For each invoice record the contract, supply, acceptance, price, payment due date, remittances, debit or credit notes and outstanding balance. Show statutory interest separately. Distinguish an admitted receivable from a contested supply, defective goods, a disputed adjustment or a claim for damages.

## Separate entitlement from procedure

Section 18 provides the statutory dispute-resolution mechanism. The original framework and the enacted 2023 and 2026 amendments must be distinguished; commencement and savings determine which process applies. Check limitation, supplier status and the chronology of registration and supplies. Do not infer a right to all Chapter V benefits merely from current registration.

## Respondent's position

A buyer should preserve documentary evidence of genuine objections, payments and adjustments. The Supreme Court in Silpi Industries recognised counterclaims and set-off in arbitration under the original Section 18 framework; the availability and merits of a particular defence are separate questions. See the official judgment and verify later treatment before applying it to a live matter.
