## Scope

Section 17 connects the unpaid price of goods or services with Section 16 interest.

## Preparing a claim

Reconcile invoices, credit notes, payments and disputed supplies. Maintain a separate calculation for principal and interest. Examine eligibility, limitation and objections independently; an invoice alone does not establish that every statutory requirement is satisfied.
