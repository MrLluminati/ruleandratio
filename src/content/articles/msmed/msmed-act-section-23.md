## Tax treatment

The provision addresses deductibility of statutory delayed-payment interest.

## Review points

Keep statutory interest separate from other expenditure and verify the applicable income-tax legislation and transitional rules for the relevant assessment period. Do not treat this provision as a complete guide to the buyer's tax obligations.
