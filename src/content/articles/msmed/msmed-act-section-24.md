## Statutory priority

Section 24 gives Sections 15–23 priority where another law is inconsistent with them.

## Interpretation

Identify the specific conflict and examine binding judicial interpretation. The clause should not be treated as eliminating every other applicable legal requirement. Read it alongside the statutory dispute-resolution mechanism and the law governing challenges.
