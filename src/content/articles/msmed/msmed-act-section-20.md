## Council establishment

Section 20 concerns State Government notifications establishing Councils and assigning their jurisdiction.

## Practical research

Locate the operative state notification, confirm the designated Council and its territorial competence, and check subsequent changes before filing. Portal availability alone does not establish legal jurisdiction.
