## Background
The buyer challenged a Facilitation Council award and sought relief from the statutory pre-deposit requirement. The court below refused waiver.

## Decision
The Supreme Court treated the seventy-five percent deposit requirement as mandatory. It distinguished dispensing with the required amount from allowing the deposit to be made by instalments in appropriate circumstances.

## Practical relevance
A challenge should address the deposit calculation, court-directed timing and any application for instalments. The deposit does not by itself establish grounds for setting aside the award or entitle the applicant to a stay. Review Sections 34 and 36 of the Arbitration Act separately.
