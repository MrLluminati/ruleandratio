## Background
The dispute concerned the deposit condition for a challenge to an MSMED award and the scope of the court's discretion over compliance.

## Decision
The Court distinguished the mandatory statutory quantum from the manner of making the deposit. The court's discretion over the manner of payment may permit instalments; it is not a general discretion to waive the statutory amount.

## Later application
Aska Equipments reiterated the mandatory character of the deposit while recognizing the court's discretion concerning instalments.
