## Questions considered
The batch examined conflicts between Chapter V of the MSMED Act and private arbitration agreements, the Council's role following unsuccessful conciliation, and supplier eligibility.

## Decision
The Court treated the statutory dispute mechanism as prevailing over inconsistent provisions of the Arbitration Act. It held that an independent arbitration agreement does not itself prevent an eligible statutory reference. It also recognized the Council's ability to arbitrate after conciliation, and the arbitral tribunal's competence to determine its jurisdiction.

## Subsequent treatment
NBCC referred the registration issue for authoritative resolution. The January 2025 Tamil Nadu Cements reference expressly raised whether the same Council members may conciliate and then arbitrate. These are issue-specific referrals, not a declaration that the entire 2022 judgment has been overruled.

## Practical relevance
Review the contract, eligibility and the actual procedural record separately. A contractual arbitration clause alone does not settle the jurisdictional question.
