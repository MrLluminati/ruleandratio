## Decision and procedural context
The Court affirmed the rejection of the writ petition challenging the Council's award because the Arbitration Act provides a statutory challenge mechanism. The Section 19 deposit condition was part of the statutory framework considered.

## Subsequent treatment
The three-judge Bench in Tamil Nadu Cements expressed reservations about an absolute exclusion of writ jurisdiction and referred questions concerning the scope of the alternative-remedy principle to five judges in January 2025.

## Practical relevance
Distinguish a challenge to an arbitral award from other Council orders or alleged jurisdictional and procedural defects. The referral itself is not an overruling of India Glycols and is not authority for an unrestricted writ remedy. Verify the current reference status and applicable binding law before advising on a filing.
