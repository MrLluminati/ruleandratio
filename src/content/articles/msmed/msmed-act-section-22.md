## Accounting disclosure

Section 22 addresses additional financial-statement disclosures by buyers subject to statutory audit.

## Review points

Reconcile outstanding amounts and interest records with the audited accounts. Consult the applicable accounting framework and current statutory requirements before relying on any particular disclosure.

## Amendment and commencement review

Section 22A is a separate enacted TReDS reporting provision. The reproduced notification identifies 15 October 2026 for its insertion. Verify covered entities and implementing rules; do not conflate its duties with Section 22.
