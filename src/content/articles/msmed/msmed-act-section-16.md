## Statutory basis

The statutory rate is three times the RBI bank rate, with monthly compounding. The start date depends on the payment obligation under Section 15.

## Calculation records

Prepare an invoice-wise schedule showing principal, acceptance dates, payment terms, payments received, and the applicable historical bank rates. Distinguish the bank rate from the repo rate. Verify the calculation method and any changes in applicable rates before quantifying a claim.
