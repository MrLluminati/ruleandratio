## Dispute resolution

The provision establishes a conciliation stage followed by arbitration if conciliation ends unsuccessfully. It also addresses jurisdiction by reference to the supplier's location.

## Preparing a reference

Identify the relevant Council, obtain its rules and filing directions, and compile the contract, registration evidence, invoices, payment reconciliation and correspondence. Verify limitation, registration timing, procedural developments and relevant case law separately. The statutory disposal period should not be presented as a guaranteed completion date.

## Amendment and commencement review

The 2026 amendment modifies the Section 18 substitution enacted by the Mediation Act, 2023. The identified October 2026 notification does not commence amending section 8. Check later notices and the independent commencement of Mediation Act section 62 before applying the revised timelines.
