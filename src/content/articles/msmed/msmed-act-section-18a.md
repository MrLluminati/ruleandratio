## Legal framework

The enacted insertion provides a land-revenue recovery route and addresses recognition of the determined debt under insolvency law. Verify commencement and the underlying Section 18 regime before invoking this route. It does not by itself establish an insolvency application's maintainability.

## Commencement research

The identified October notification does not appoint a commencement date for this amending provision. Authenticate the original notification and check later notifications and implementing rules before asserting present applicability.
