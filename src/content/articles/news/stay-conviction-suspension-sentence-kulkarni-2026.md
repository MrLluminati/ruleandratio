## What happened

On 25 November 2026, the Supreme Court allowed challenges to Karnataka High Court orders suspending sentences and, in one instance, staying a conviction. The Bench comprised Justices Prashant Kumar Mishra and Shree Chandrashekhar.

## Legal significance

Staying a conviction requires a more demanding merits assessment than suspending a sentence pending appeal. For the former, the Court described an obvious and fundamental defect in the conviction judgment as the relevant standard. Possible legislative disqualification was insufficient as the principal reason for relief.

For sentence suspension, the Court emphasized reasoned consideration of the prospects of appeal and seriousness of the case. Delay in hearing an appeal was not an automatic entitlement to release.

## Disposition

The challenged orders were set aside and the respondents directed to surrender within two weeks. The judgment concerns interim appellate relief, not disposal of the underlying conviction appeals.
