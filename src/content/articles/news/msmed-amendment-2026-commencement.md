## What changed

The amending Act was published on 13 August 2026. It revises registration and classification provisions, provides for invoice settlement through TReDS in specified procurement settings, and alters the delayed-payment dispute machinery. The text also provides for enforcement of settlements and awards as land-revenue arrears, and additional directions for release of deposited amounts during award challenges.

## Commencement matters

Section 1(2) permits separate commencement dates. An indexed reproduction of S.O. 5575(E), dated 12 October 2026, identifies 15 October for sections 2–7, 11 and 13–17, and 15 December for section 12. Sections 8–10 are not listed in that reproduction. The original commencement notification and any subsequent notifications must be obtained before concluding what is operative.

## Practical significance

The new dispute timelines, enforcement mechanism and changes to Section 19 cannot be assumed to apply merely because Parliament passed the Act. Check the separate commencement of the Mediation Act amendments as well, and the saving provisions for pending matters.
