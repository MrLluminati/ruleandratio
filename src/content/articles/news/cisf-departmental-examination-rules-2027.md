## What happened

The Ministry of Home Affairs published rules on 25 November 2026 for the UPSC's 2027 limited departmental competitive examination for CISF Assistant Commandant (Executive) vacancies.

## Eligibility and procedure

The rules require four years of regular service in specified Sub-Inspector or Inspector ranks by 1 January 2027, a recognized university degree and the prescribed disciplinary record. They prescribe age eligibility assessed on 1 August 2027, specified SC/ST relaxation and a maximum of three attempts. Applications must follow the departmental channel.

## Pending litigation

Final selection is conditional on the outcome of Diwakar Pande & Ors. v. Ministry of Home Affairs & Ors., W.P.(C) 5877/2022, in the Delhi High Court. Rule 12 also requires an opportunity for written representation before penalties under that rule.

Vacancies and examination dates will be specified separately by UPSC. The Gazette is not itself the examination notice.
